Flowchart · GPT preset

Routing and failure recovery

Decision nodes are rounded rectangles that size to their label instead of huge diamonds.

flowchart TD
  classDef danger fill:#fee2e2,stroke:#dc2626,stroke-width:1.5px,color:#7f1d1d
  classDef success fill:#dcfce7,stroke:#16a34a,stroke-width:1.5px,color:#14532d

  A(["客户端请求"]) --> B{"命中缓存?"}
  B -->|"是"| C(["直接返回缓存"]):::success
  B -->|"否"| D["调用上游模型"]
  D --> E{"上游 5xx?"}
  E -->|"是"| F["指数退避重试"]:::danger
  E -->|"否"| G(["流式返回客户端"]):::success
  F -. "最多 3 次" .-> D
  F -->|"预算耗尽"| H["返回降级提示"]:::danger
Sequence · soft theme
